# H13 - one policy for every extra round

## Acceptance criterion
A single pure policy governs every round beyond the model's own tool call
(auto-continue paths, completion engine): every extra round records its cause
and the budget it consumed; unknown causes are refused; a setting falls back to
the legacy behaviour (default on only once equivalence tests pass; otherwise
the policy stays in shadow, documented).

## What already existed
Per-path counters and limits inside the loop (length continues, unknown-tool
nudges, rejections, tests/static/syntax fix rounds, completion continues,
step-budget extensions), each with its own `used < limit` test, and
`harness_check` events without a common cause.

## What was added
- `src/extra_round_policy.py` (pure): the closed `CAUSES` registry (33 causes in
  five families: budget, transport, correction, verification, completion),
  `decide_extra_round`, `ExtraRounds` (modes `legacy` / `shadow` / `enforce`,
  receipts with `cause`, `family`, `budget_used`, `budget_limit`,
  `budget_remaining`, `extra_rounds_total`; unknown causes recorded and, under
  enforce, refused; `preview`, `event`, `extension_event`, `summary`).
- `src/agent_loop.py`: the extra-round sites ask `_xr.permits(cause, used,
  limit)` with the counter and limit each path already had, and spread the
  receipt into the event they already emitted. The limits are the old
  constants and settings, unchanged.
- Setting `agent_extra_round_policy` (default `enforce`) in `DEFAULT_SETTINGS`
  and `src/agent_settings_schema.py`.

## Tests
`tests/test_extra_round_policy.py` (46):
`test_the_policy_equals_the_legacy_used_below_limit_rule_for_every_cause`
(equivalence over every cause and a 6x6 budget grid),
`test_an_unknown_cause_is_refused[*]`, `test_enforce_applies_the_policy_and_refuses_unknown_causes`,
`test_shadow_keeps_the_legacy_answer_and_records_a_divergence`,
`test_legacy_mode_is_the_old_rule_and_records_nothing`,
`test_every_cause_the_loop_names_is_registered`,
`test_every_registered_cause_is_used_by_the_loop`,
`test_every_extra_round_site_goes_through_the_policy` (AST audit of the loop:
each place that announces a further round records it in the same block),
`test_the_audit_itself_notices_a_round_with_no_record`,
`test_every_auto_continue_event_reports_its_cause`;
`tests/test_agent_loop_characterization.py::test_the_legacy_mode_keeps_the_same_rounds_without_the_receipts`,
`::test_the_shadow_mode_grants_what_legacy_grants_and_records_the_decision`, and the
per-scenario assertions on `extra_rounds()`; `tests/test_continuation_decision_shadow.py`.

## Evidence
The equivalence test shows the policy grants exactly when the legacy rule does
for every registered cause, so `enforce` differs only for an unregistered cause,
which the AST audits show the loop never names. The characterization
scenarios run in legacy, shadow and enforce and produce the same rounds.

## Remaining limits
- `context_overflow_compact` is counted by the stuck-watch (`_stuck`), which
  owns that budget; the policy receives its counter and limit but does not own
  the counter.
- `loop_recovery` records the cause; its own limit lives with the loop breaker
  and is passed in, not defined here.
- The completion engine decides in its own module; its continue round is one
  cause (`completion_continue`) in the policy, with the engine's budget as the
  limit. Its internal frontier decisions are not re-expressed here.
