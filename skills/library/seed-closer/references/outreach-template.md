# Outreach template (seed-closer)

Placeholders: {first_name}, {firm}, {personal_line}, {startup}, {one_liner_sentence}, {tagline}, {raise}, {stage}, {sender} (the founder's real name), {title}, {company_address}, {intro_subject}, {intro_day}, {extra}, {ask}. The opt-out line and the signature go in every message. Keep {personal_line} in the intro. Follow-ups stay under 80 words before the opt-out line.

Opt-out line (use these words): `Not a fit? Reply 'no' and I won't follow up.`

Signature: {sender}, {title}, {startup}, {company_address}. Use the founder's real name; if it is a placeholder such as "Your Name", stop and ask. Never invent a sign-off.

## intro

Subject: {startup}: {tagline}

Hi {first_name},

{personal_line}

{startup} is {one_liner_sentence}.

We're raising a {raise} {stage}. Open to a 20-minute call next week?

{optout}

{signature}

## follow-up 1 (3 business days after the intro, if no reply)

Subject: Re: {intro_subject}

Hi {first_name},

Bumping this up: my note from {intro_day} about {startup}, {one_liner_sentence}. {extra}

Open to 20 minutes next week?

{optout}

{signature}

`{extra}` is one optional line with something new and checkable (a demo link, a metric from `seed/model.md` or the pilot).

## follow-up 2 (7 business days after the intro, last touch)

Subject: Re: {intro_subject}

Hi {first_name},

Quick one, and my last note on this: {ask}

{optout}

{signature}

`{ask}` is a single concrete question, for example whether another person at the firm covers this space.

## What counts as a real personal line

One sentence, at least about seven words, naming something from that investor's public record (a deal, a post, a sector, a number) with its source kept in `seed/investors.md`. Refuse: empty, cut off with "...", a placeholder, a stock opener ("hope this finds you well", "big fan", "love what you're doing", "impressive portfolio", "came across your profile"), the same line for two people, and a line that names nothing from their row.
