# Term sheet flags (seed-terms)

For each clause found, give: the excerpt, severity, one plain-words sentence on what it means, and the exact question for the lawyer. Severity: **high** means push back; **medium** means understand it and probably negotiate; **low** means common, know it. The assignment below is a working default, not a ruling; a clause the list does not cover is not "safe".

| clause | what to look for | default severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liquidation preference | a multiple over 1x | high |
| Participating preferred | investors take the preference and share in the rest, capped or not | high |
| Anti-dilution | full ratchet | high; narrow-based weighted average medium |
| Redemption | investors can force the company to buy shares back | high |
| Cumulative dividends | dividends accrue and are paid before common | medium |
| Board | investors control the board (count the seats) | high |
| Protective provisions | vetoes that reach into the budget, hiring or debt | medium |
| Pay-to-play | investors who skip a round lose their preferred rights | medium |
| Drag-along | the preferred alone can force a sale | medium |
| No-shop | over 30 days is medium; 60 days or more is high | medium / high |
| Deadline | an offer that expires in 48 hours or less | high |
| Founder vesting | a vesting reset on shares already earned | high |
| Super pro rata | rights to buy more than their share in later rounds | medium |
| MFN | most-favoured-nation terms across other investors | low |
| Option pool | a new or enlarged pool counted in the pre-money | medium |

Standard, listed as standard when found: 1x non-participating preference, broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution, no redemption, a founder-controlled or balanced board, a no-shop of 30 days or less.

Always end with: a startup lawyer should read the document before anything is signed. Do not say "sign it" or call the term sheet good or bad overall.
